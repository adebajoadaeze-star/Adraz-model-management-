About Adraz

Faces made for your vision.

Adraz Model Management is a facial modelling agency connecting creatives and brands with talent for beauty, commercial and editorial projects. Based in Ibadan and serving Abeokuta, we make it easier to discover and book models suited to your creative brief. From beauty campaigns and makeup portfolios to brand content and editorial shoots, we help bring ideas to life through the right faces, professional communication and a seamless booking experience.
